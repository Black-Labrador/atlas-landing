Without hedging

A lithium producer in Chile needs $100M to expand. Western banks say: "How do we know you can service the debt if lithium drops 60%?" Without a hedge, the bank says no — or demands 15%+ interest. The producer's only option is a Chinese offtaker who'll finance in exchange for 80% of production at a steep discount. Locked in. Single buyer dependency.

With Atlas

The same producer hedges 60% of output at a locked-in price on Atlas. Goes back to the bank: "Revenue is locked in. Here's the position." Bank lends at 9%, lighter covenants, bigger facility. The producer saves $6M/year in financing costs and keeps their offtake freedom. No Chinese dependency.

"Policy secures supply. Atlas makes it tradable."

Governments are channeling hundreds of billions into critical-mineral supply chains — through the IRA, the EU Critical Raw Materials Act, the CHIPS Act and a wave of strategic offtake and stockpiling programs — to reduce dependence on a single processing power. But capital without market infrastructure is capital without leverage. Atlas builds the missing financial layer: a transparent reference and a place to price, hedge and finance it.

The Oracle

Price discovery, from scratch

No existing benchmark serves frontier producers. We built one — aggregating from the same sources the physical market trusts, with the transparency crypto demands.

A blend, not a single feed

The Atlas oracle blends multiple independent reference inputs per mineral into one continuous, manipulation-resistant price — FX-normalized to USD, tiered by source quality, and gated by staleness checks, deviation clamps and circuit breakers. Atlas's own venue data is deliberately excluded so the reference never becomes self-referential.

  • Multi-source tiered blend with quality weighting
  • Median aggregation + outlier rejection
  • FX normalization to USD/tonne
  • Staleness gates & deviation circuit breakers
  • Perp-implied price discovery layered on top
